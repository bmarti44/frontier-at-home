RED confirmation: 2026-07-28

Scoring exceptions emit a complete failure triplet, but scheduler failures
after output creation do not. Archive errors, three-relay failure and
systemd-run failure can leave an unlabeled partial attempt.

The new contract requires an EXIT trap from output creation until successful
unit scheduling. It calls the fixed preflight-failure writer and disarms only
after systemd-run succeeds. The contract is RED because the scheduler has no
such trap or preflight-failure command.
