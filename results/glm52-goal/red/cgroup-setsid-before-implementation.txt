The unchanged PGID-only wrapper was given an approved candidate that waited
for attestation, forked, called setsid(), recorded its exact PID, and slept.

The wrapper returned zero while the recorded S-state child remained alive
outside the original process group. This is AR-064.

Acceptance requires a dedicated cgroup whose control-group stop reaps the
setsid descendant, plus finite memory.high/memory.max, zero swap, and
memory.oom.group=1.
