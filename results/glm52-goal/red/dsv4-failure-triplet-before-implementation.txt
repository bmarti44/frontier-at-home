Reviewed candidate: 546637a

If startup, probe, or engine-log collection fails before all four stage files
exist, build_observation raises and the exception handler writes summary.json
only. That violates the mandatory preservation of every failed/null attempt.
The successful manifest is also mislabeled as GLM W11 even though it is the
DeepSeek reference profile.

The new test requires every early exception to emit manifest.json, raw.jsonl,
and summary.json, with FAIL, a context_failure raw record, and the separate
dsv4_reference_context gate marked as non-authoritative until ingested by a
registered controller path.
