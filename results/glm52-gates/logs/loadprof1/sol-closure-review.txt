VERDICT: NOT SUPPORTED

1. Replay inertness does not imply append inertness. Byte-identical greedy output proves only that this particular replay produced the same selected tokens. It does not prove identical logits, structural unreadability of the differing rows, or inertness for different suffix content and positions. Append queries may select or weight those rows differently. The 22–29-token append also exercises a different batching regime than the 3-token replay suffix.

2. The harness does not capture the claimed no-evaluation-between round trip. rowdump-001 is taken at replay sync entry, post-load/pre-evaluation; rowdump-002 is taken at the subsequent dummy request, after the replay suffix and generation have been evaluated. Thus evaluation occurs between the compared dumps. The L40 changes could have arisen during replay evaluation, potentially after the relevant output computations. This does not isolate serialization/restoration damage.

3. The raw log confirms that the evicted and loaded snapshots are different checkpoints: it stores live tokens=5063, then loads the existing tokens=5044 shard. Comparing their lower rows may be informative, but it is not a direct dump-before-store/load-same-file/dump-after-load experiment of the invariant requested by the prior audit.

4. “Identical pattern and count” is overstated. invariant-closure.txt contains only per-segment byte counts. rowdump_replay.sh calculates Hamming counts but neither compares differing-byte coordinates nor hashes the masks/deltas against the append flow. Equal counts could reflect fixed-size conversion, deterministic evaluation-time mutation, padding/layout behavior, or two distinct mechanisms with the same aggregate count.

5. One run is enough to establish the narrow existential result: an L40 dump difference and identical replay text occurred together once. It is not enough for the universal claim that the rows cannot affect append divergence or for a serving-policy change. Fresh-server repetitions are useful, but more important are branch-matched 22–29-token suffix probes, varied suffix contents/checkpoints, and logit comparisons. Incidentally, the script requests and the live=5063 trace indicates 16 generated tokens, not one; that strengthens the narrow observation but does not fix the generalization.

6. Additional methodology gaps remain. The script assumes dump-number/request association, binary layout, segment offsets, and that the first half of each eight-row segment maps exactly to positions 5040–5043. It asserts none of these. It also does not verify equal/full dump lengths; zip silently truncates. Only layers 0 and 40 and four pre-resume rows are examined. The committed raw excerpt lacks dump timestamps, sync traces, sizes, coordinate masks, and the append-flow comparison needed to independently audit those assumptions.

The evidence improves the state of knowledge to: “the observed L40 difference did not change greedy output in one short exact-replay execution.” It does not close the same-lineage round-trip invariant and does not exclude those differences, or a related L40 mechanism, from affecting longer/different append evaluations. The strict guard therefore should not yet be relaxed on this evidence.
